A fest as a comic book

Revelations is an intra-department fest at CHRIST University. Postgraduate students compete in technical and non-technical events, and the 2024 theme was the Marvel universe.

Students were split into seven leagues: Iron Man, Captain America, Thor, Black Widow, Scarlet Witch, Spider-Man and Doctor Strange. More than 300 competed across ten events, with preliminaries from 23 March and finals on 13 April. Every event got a Marvel name, like Ultron’s Ultimate Quest for UI/UX.

So the site reads like a comic book:

  • black and white comic pages behind everything;
  • Bangers for headings, Comic Neue for text;
  • thick black borders and hard offset shadows;
  • pink name plates and halftone yellow event tiles;
  • a title that cycles between white, red and yellow.

Colour you have to earn

The league panels start in greyscale. Hover over one and it slowly floods with its hero’s colour over ten seconds: red for Thor, yellow, deep blue, and so on. It turns browsing the leagues into a small reveal, and keeps the page calm until someone picks a side.

Every page also has its own accent. The current link in the bar is underlined in that page’s colour: red for events, blue for the schedule, yellow for the team.

Data in, pages out

All the content lives in typed arrays: events, the schedule and the team. Every page is a map over them, so the committee’s last-minute changes were data edits, not design work.

The comic panel effect is almost entirely CSS: greyscale panels of different widths that fade to their hero’s colour on hover.

Looking back, I’d tighten a few things: typed props instead of any, one modal layout instead of separate desktop and phone copies, and a designed 404 page.

By the numbers

  • 7 leagues and 10 events.
  • 3 competition days with 18 scheduled rounds.
  • A team page covering faculty, core team, committee and event heads.
  • More than 300 students competing.
